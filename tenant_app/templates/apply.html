How to Apply

Already applied? Check your status.

Applications for our affordable-housing properties are submitted on paper. Here's how it works:

  1. Download and print the application below.
  2. Fill it out by hand, in black or blue ink, one letter or number per box.
  3. Sign and date it, then bring or mail it to the property you're applying to, or to our leasing office.
  4. Staff will scan your form and process your application. They'll give you a reference number.
  5. Use that reference number and your email to check your status any time using the link above.
